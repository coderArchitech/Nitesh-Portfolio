Sidekick

A private, real-time AI copilot for meetings on Teams, Zoom, Meet and Webex.

It transcribes the call, detects questions (especially ones that use your name), and suggests answers with sources from your documents, the meeting so far, and your screen. Answers appear in an overlay that is hidden from screen sharing.

View on GitHub Decision records
  • Retrieval-grounded answers: hybrid vector and keyword search over your documents, with numbered citations
  • Privacy by design: consent before listening, encrypted local storage (SQLCipher), keys in the OS keychain, no hosted backend
  • Pluggable providers: Deepgram, AssemblyAI, Soniox or local Whisper; Claude, OpenAI, Gemini or Ollama
  • Meeting memory: agenda tracking, “what did I miss?” recaps, and action items with owners

How it's built: a Rust workspace of 15 crates with one crate per module, 15 architecture decision records, typed IPC, CI on three operating systems, and per-stage latency targets.

Flipbook

A publishing studio that turns a product catalog into a shoppable 3D magazine and a PDF.

You build pages from templates in a visual editor and publish an immutable edition. Readers turn its pages in the browser, and you can export the same content as an A4 PDF. It runs as one Node.js container with SQLite and needs no cloud accounts.

View on GitHub Watch the demo
  • Visual editor: layouts, product cards, hotspots, layers, undo, autosave with conflict protection, and review and approval
  • 3D reader: curved WebGL pages you can drag to turn, a 2D fallback, search, zoom, RTL and phone support
  • PDF export: a background worker writes A4 PDFs with embedded fonts and working links, without a headless browser
  • Secure by default: scrypt hashing, CSRF and origin checks, per-account isolation, and a read-only container

How it's built: a PRD and a technical design with HLD, LLD and API specs. A durable SQLite job queue, and unit, integration and Playwright tests on desktop and phone.
